# Privacy Policy — Stellar Logbook

*Unofficial fan tool for Elite: Dangerous. Not affiliated with Frontier Developments.*

**The short version:** your exploration data never leaves your phone, except for
the specific API calls the app makes to community services to look up public
galaxy data.

## What the app stores

- **On this device only:** your visited systems, jumps, discoveries, notes and
  bookmarks (a local Room database); your sync preferences and the current
  "target" system (Android DataStore); your EDSM API key (Android Keystore-
  backed encrypted storage).
- **Optional:** the *Uncharted Pack* — a public, anonymous spatial index of
  known systems (~75 MB slice for your region) downloaded from GitHub Releases.

## What leaves the device

- **EDSM (edsm.net):** your commander name + API key are sent to EDSM's API to
  fetch *your own* flight logs. No other personal data is sent. EDSM is a
  third-party community service; see its own privacy policy.
- **Spansh (spansh.co.uk):** public system lookups by id64 (no credentials).
- **GitHub:** update checks (release metadata) and pack downloads.

The app does **not** contain ads, analytics, crash reporting, or trackers. It
does **not** upload your journals anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** EDSM/Spansh/GitHub calls.
- **Notifications:** only for the target-system reminder you explicitly set.
- **Install unknown apps:** only when you choose to install an update the app
  downloaded from the project's GitHub Releases.

## Community data

Galaxy data comes from the Elite Dangerous community: EDSM, Spansh, EDDN and
EDDiscovery. All credit to those projects and the commanders who report data.
If you use EDDiscovery you can also publish your own events to EDDN and give
back to the community — that is opt-in, in EDDiscovery, not in this app.
